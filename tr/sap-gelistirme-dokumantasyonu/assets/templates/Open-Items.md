Use stable OI IDs and explicit statuses: open, investigating, blocked, resolved, or accepted-risk. An accepted risk needs the actual acceptance authority and evidence. Dates and owners may be unknown; do not invent them.

## Resolved items

Retain the original ID, resolution, closure date, and evidence here or in a linked decision. Record reopening as a new event, preserving the prior closure.
